Reduction to the essential part

Takes a known mark and strips it to one feature: the Toyota oval becomes only the inner T with clipped arcs; Tesla keeps only the T wedge.

Construction lines on show

Thin dashed guides at 0, 90 and 45 degrees run through the centre and edges of the mark; the solid mark sits in one colour on an off-white field.

03

Letter becomes symbol

Letterforms carry the idea: FIFA A loses its crossbar, DUBAI letters stretch into a skyline, Coric Arhitektur merges A and C into one monogram.

04

Flat fill finals, outline sketches

Final marks are solid single colour fills. Outlines appear only in the sketch phase (Nike, Lacoste traced as outlines in Illustrator).

NikeLacoste
05

Sharp angled cuts

Speed is drawn with slanted, sheared terminals: F1, SHARMAX, the thin stroke Mercedes star.
